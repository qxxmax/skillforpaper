# Venue Profile: SciPost Physics

## Identity

- Venue: SciPost Physics
- Track/section: regular article
- Guideline URL: <https://scipost.org/SciPostPhys/authoring> and
  <https://scipost.org/FAQ>
- Guideline fetch date: 2026-08-12 (direct fetch bot-blocked; facts from
  substitute search snippets of scipost.org pages — weaker evidence than a
  full page render; re-verify at portal before actual submission)
- Deadline / submission window: rolling
- Expected decision timeline: ~10 weeks average submission-to-publication
  (third-party index; confirm at portal)

## Format Rules

| item | value | source anchor |
|---|---|---|
| main-text limit | no strict limit; "strict minimum necessary for peers to reproduce the research" | SciPostPhys/authoring |
| template | SciPost LaTeX style preferred; general LaTeX accepted | SciPostPhys/authoring |
| reproducibility | acceptance criteria require reproducibility-enabling resources (protocols, data, code) | scipost acceptance criteria |

## Review Model

- Blind model: open peer-witnessed refereeing; reports posted publicly after
  editorial vetting; referees may stay anonymous per report
- Rebuttal window: authors reply publicly to each report; community comments
  possible
- Revision model: resubmission rounds; all raised points must be addressed
  before an acceptance recommendation

## Policy

- Checklist required: no formal checklist; acceptance criteria act as the
  rubric (clarity, reproducibility, complete citations, objective
  conclusions with limitations)
- Code/data submission policy: reproducibility resources expected directly
  or via linked repositories
- Preprint (arXiv) policy: submission via preprint server is a supported
  pathway
- Exclusivity: must not be under consideration elsewhere
- Cost: two-way open access; no author charges

## Fit

- Scope-fit statement: SciPost Physics accepts full-length methods papers
  judged on reproducibility and objective claim boundaries — matching the
  Part 2 review's verdict wording ("adaptation, not invention"; scoped
  numerical claims).
- Main risk at this venue: public reports mean every wording bound in
  `claim_evidence_ledger.md` will be stress-tested in the open; the
  unresolved code-availability item becomes visible immediately.
