# Search Scope

## Relevant Item
A scholarly work is relevant if it contributes (1) a learned/neural sampler for an unnormalized target, (2) a learned sampler for lattice field theory, or (3) a correction/exactness mechanism materially used by such samplers.

## Inclusion
- Proposal/transport/path/flow/diffusion/control samplers trained without requiring normalized target density.
- Lattice scalar, gauge, or fermion field applications using learned generative or proposal models.
- Mechanisms including MH correction, importance/AIS weights, sequential resampling, stochastic path-work/Jarzynski-type weights, or exact reweighting.

## Exclusion
- Pure density estimation; generic generative modeling without unnormalized-target sampling.
- Classical methods with no direct explanatory role for learned samplers.
- Duplicate manifestations; unverifiable records are retained as uncertain, not asserted.

## Objective And Stop Standard
High-recall scoped landscape under 40 calls. Stop with known risk after multi-channel lexical/domain/bibliographic routes, G1 backward/forward and author/method expansion where accessible, at least three representatives per major facet or explicit gap, six integrity-checked PDFs, and an adversarial pass. No completeness claim.
